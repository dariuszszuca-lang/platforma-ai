What people ask before the first call.

We are in the US. Does the time difference work?

Yes. I keep 11:00 to 14:00 New York time open for calls, which is 17:00 to 20:00 in Poland. Outside those hours, email gets an answer within one business day.

Do you work in English?

Yes. Calls, documents, the system interface and the handover are in English.

Our spreadsheet is free.

A spreadsheet is free only while your time is free. A system starts to pay off when follow ups, documents and statuses go missing, and one person has to ask another where a client stands.

Why not an off the shelf CRM?

Because you pay every month for every seat, and you still bend your process to fit the tool. Here you pay once, and the tool fits the process. The numbers are on the systems page.

What if you disappear?

Code ownership and access are agreed before we start. If the code sits in your repository, any developer can pick it up. No dependency on one person.

How fast is it really?

A CRM or mini-system takes 1 to 2 weeks. A business system takes 2 to 3 months. Both start with the same 30 minute call.
